Connections & field notesMIDI / how to play

Bring your keyboard

Touch and computer keys work without hardware. USB MIDI is optional and depends on browser support. Connect your Akai by USB, switch its internal sounds off, then connect here. Audio comes from this device’s speakers or headphones.

MIDI permission is requested only when you choose Connect MIDI.

Previously assigned MIDI knobs still work. The eight macro panels and Learn buttons have been removed from the interface.

Play, shape, record

Play any key to start audio. Vintage sound lab selects a new keyboard sound; these are original, vintage-inspired patches, not samples or exact branded reproductions. The eleven original sounds remain in the Sound menu. Tap Laser Zap and Crystal FM; hold a key for wind, waves, chorus and Cinema Convergence.

Sustain holds released notes. Drone toggles each note. Fade out silences the whole synth, including echoes and held notes, over the selected duration. A new note cancels the fade. Fade does not stop recording. Panic clears notes and tails immediately.

Slower and Faster change pitch and timing together like tape. Record, stop recording, then download WAV for TCK03. Nothing is uploaded; download takes before leaving. Mobile uses four voices; desktop uses eight.
