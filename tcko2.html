Connections & field notesMIDI / how to play

Bring your keyboard

Touch and computer keys work without hardware. USB MIDI is optional and depends on browser support. Connect your Akai by USB, switch its internal sounds off, then connect here. Audio comes from this device’s speakers or headphones.

MIDI permission is requested only when you choose Connect MIDI.

Previously assigned MIDI knobs still work. The eight macro panels and Learn buttons have been removed from the interface.

Play, layer, loop

Keep playing is on by default: released notes keep their original held-key sound, without adding Drone or Bloom. Tap a held key again to release it. Selecting another sound keeps the previous instrument, Glitch, Fragments, pitch and echo chain running independently; play new keys for the new sound. Turning on Glitch or Fragments, or changing an enabled Glitch type, keeps the old layer and starts the new effect from its held notes. Sliders, effect Off and pitch edit only the current layer. Live volume controls all live layers.

The × sound buttons remove individual kept layers with a short fade; Clear kept layers leaves current keys and the loop alone. Desktop supports four independent live layers and eight voices total; compact/mobile supports two layers and four voices. At capacity, new layers/notes are refused rather than cutting off the old ones. Use a loop to hold more recorded layers. Keep playing Off releases its held notes naturally and restores ordinary key release. Sustain and Drone remain available.

Make loop starts capturing up to 30 seconds of all live layers, including their effects. Loop it repeats that phrase while the live keyboard stays independent. Add layer captures exactly one more loop cycle and merges it into the loop; playback is never recorded back into itself. Pause loop leaves live notes alone. Clear loop deletes only that loop. Loop volume controls the recorded bed. Pitch and effect changes do not alter the captured loop.

Loops stay in this tab only: use Record and Stop recording to save the combined performance to your browser’s recent takes for TCKO3, or Download WAV for a backup. Overdubs are merged stereo audio, not separate TCK03 tracks. Keep separate WAV takes if you want independent mixing later.

Fade out fades every live layer and pauses the loop without deleting it. A new key cancels an unfinished fade. Panic and Stop silence all live layers immediately and keep the loop paused. Switching tabs pauses everything for safety. The eleven original sounds and eight vintage-inspired patches remain; these are original synthesis designs, not exact branded reproductions.
