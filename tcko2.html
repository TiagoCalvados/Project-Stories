Connections & field notesMIDI / how to play

Bring your keyboard

Touch and computer keys work without hardware. USB MIDI is optional and depends on browser support. Connect your Akai by USB, switch its internal sounds off, then connect here. Audio comes from this device’s speakers or headphones.

MIDI permission is requested only when you choose Connect MIDI.

Previously assigned MIDI knobs still work. The eight macro panels and Learn buttons have been removed from the interface.

Play, layer, loop

Stack sounds is on by default. Start with a sound, then choose another main sound, Sound Technology or Old Electro patch: each distinct selection stays in the mix. All enabled layers follow the same piano, computer keys and MIDI. Adding a sound while you hold a chord brings it into that chord immediately; the earlier sounds continue. Selecting a sound already in the mix selects that layer without duplicating it.

Glitch and Fragments process the actual sum of the keyboard layers. Choosing a Glitch type turns it on. Strength blends the effect and Volume adjusts its wet signal. The × chips remove individual sounds with a short fade. Clear earlier layers retains the selected sound. Stack sounds Off replaces only the selected layer on later selections. Sustain and Drone hold notes; ordinary key release releases them. Panic, Stop and leaving the tab stop notes but retain your sound choices.

Desktop supports eight sound layers and sixteen simultaneous voices across them. Compact/mobile supports four layers and eight voices. A selection or note that exceeds capacity is refused; earlier sounds are kept. Your stack and settings stay in this browser without autoplay.

Make loop captures up to 30 seconds of the combined live layers and effects. Loop it repeats the phrase while the live keyboard stays independent; Add layer overdubs one cycle. Loop volume controls the recorded bed. REC captures the live layered mix, the loop and opened sound. Download WAV for a backup or open the take in TCKO3. Loops and recent takes remain on this device.

Fade out fades all live sound and pauses the loop; a new note cancels an unfinished fade. The eleven original sounds, eight Old Electro patches and twelve sound technologies are original synthesis designs.
